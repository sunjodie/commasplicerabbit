# Chapter 3 — The Wrong Path

Twenty narrative paintings in five 2×2 atlases, using Chapter 1's sheet-02 and the approved Chapter 2 character artwork as visual references. Generated with the built-in image tool; exact prompts are in `prompts.json`, with source provenance in `source-map.json`.

Each cell is a 3:2 painting. WebP encoding preserves the full 1536×1024 atlas at quality 94. The existing frame painter isolates each cell for desktop, tablet, mobile and exploration views. Four narrative anchors per scene select views as the prose advances; responsive text grouping can skip an intermediate view. Questions and retries use the final cell of their scene. The two wide views with rabbits on the left use a left-biased portrait crop to keep them visible.

1. Beyond the shed: wide path, low lamplight, map discussion, hurrying ahead.
2. The hollow knocking: bridge approach, uneasy reaction, cape rescue, crumbling bank.
3. Where Poppy had stood: retreat, muddy lamp, apology, shared map.
4. Listening again: discussion, uncertainty, wide stream, listening reaction.
5. The higher path: fingerpost, blocked path, waiting, return towards the village.

Review: Poppy retains cream fur, plum cape, rust scarf and brown satchel; Willow retains grey-brown fur and a moss-green coat. Ear silhouettes were reviewed for duplicated ears; some ears are naturally occluded in close-ups. Poppy holds the oil lamp. Willow's extinguished paper lantern stays hidden. No legible notes, map labels, identifiable culprit or revealing brace-cut detail appears. The aftermath sheet was corrected to keep the rabbits standing, remove an invented stone bridge, and restore Poppy's upright ear in the rear view.
