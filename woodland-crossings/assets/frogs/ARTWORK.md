# Painted frog atlas

Asset: `assets/frogs/painted-frogs.webp`. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool; alpha preserved. The generated PNG was resized to a 1000×400 WebP atlas for delivery. Ten cells, five columns and two rows, in the order used by frogs.js. Existing scene paintings were used only as visual style references; they were not modified. Collection identities and language notes are unchanged.

The visible scene sprites use smaller natural proportions, subdued light and colour, no background or hover badge, and default cursor. Invisible 44px targets and keyboard focus outlines remain. The point stored for each frog marks its feet on the source painting; cover-crop geometry keeps the point on the same ledge, stone or timber when the viewport changes. Later chapter frogs appear in their establishing paintings rather than floating over every storyboard crop. Searches reopen those same paintings.

## Final generation prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE production sprite atlas of 10 tiny natural-looking woodland frogs for a dark autumn storybook mystery, transparent background. Layout is an EXACT evenly spaced 5-column by 2-row grid, ten equal square cells, one complete isolated frog centered in each cell, feet aligned at 80% of each cell height, generous transparent padding. Atlas aspect ratio 5:2, preferably 2000x800. Not an icon sheet: these are realistic painterly miniature animals that must blend into detailed rain-soaked stone, moss, timber and candlelit foliage. Style richly textured illustrated naturalism, soft brush edges, muted olive/brown mottled skin, small realistic amber eyes, subdued warm side lighting, low contrast, folded frog legs, grounded crouching poses in three-quarter or side profile, varied facing direction. No outlines, no giant cartoon eyes, no white highlights, no halos, no shadows outside small feet, no scenery or platforms, no labels or text, no borders. Each frog occupies roughly middle 65% cell width and 60% cell height. Tiny understated accessories in order left to right: top row 1 faded brownish burgundy ribbon tied around neck, 2 fine tarnished wire spectacles, 3 little closed brown book beside forefeet, 4 very small folded ochre umbrella resting at side (not a canopy), 5 an old dry leaf lying on head. Bottom row 6 slender reed at side, 7 small weathered brown cap, 8 small folded parchment map beside forefeet, 9 faded dark wool scarf, 10 tiny clover stem near forefeet. Frogs must read as part of a moody painted environment at 20–30 pixels tall rather than as UI mascots. Transparent alpha everywhere beyond each frog and its tiny accessory. Use the recent game scene images only as mood guidance, do not reproduce any characters or scene.
