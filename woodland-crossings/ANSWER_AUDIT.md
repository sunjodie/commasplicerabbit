# Answer ambiguity audit — 30 October 2026

Scope: all 30 main grammar questions, 30 recovery questions, three bonus questions, 10 supporting notebook tasks, nine clue-notebook working pages and the Chapter 3 reflection.

Standard: one grammatically and contextually defensible answer among the choices actually displayed. A narrower instruction does not justify rejecting another grammatical repair.

## Implementation

Each editable join has its own small menu. Correct comma/full-stop positions offer comma, full stop and nor. Conjunction/semicolon positions offer their target, comma and nor; nor targets offer comma and nor, with the existing structured inversion exercise retained. Menus exclude other valid boundary repairs and contrast synonyms. All questions start with comma joins; pre-existing full-stop answers are no longer displayed as an alternative correct starting state.

Changed m24/r24 and m29/r29 to remove pre-existing conjunctions at editable main-clause joins: otherwise a full stop before the conjunction could also work. Main m24 now adds but, recovery r24 adds and, and both m29 versions add but at that join. These retain the same resulting prose and story meaning.

## Verification and limits

Manual structural review of the actual sentences and distractors accompanies exhaustive enumeration of all 2,075 offered grammar combinations. For each of the 63 questions, exactly one combination is both marked grammatical by the authored data and accepted by the checker. Enumeration checks data consistency; it is not an independent natural-language grammar judge. Existing tests cover supporting-task evidence pairs, notebook links, all chapter completion paths, recovery, rewards and saves. New UI tests verify the per-join menus for mixed questions. Legacy selections of retired tools reset on the same question without losing progress or support status. No artwork or story narration changed. Physical-device and live deployment verification remain outstanding.
